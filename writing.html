On ChatGPT

ChatGPT is a prediction machine—and what it predicts is the next probable string of words in a phrase. Although this can produce grammatically fluent text, it lacks access to the most vital part of language: How words relate to the outside world.

That’s where you shine as a writer.

Here’s one area where copywriters can outperform ChatGPT:

Metaphors—our cognitive ability to understand one idea in terms of another. Metaphors reflect something deep about the way we juggle concepts in our minds; they are also crucial for communicating ideas to others.

What does this mean for models like GPT-4, which lack internal concepts? My prediction is that, aside from producing shallow comparisons, metaphors are a permanent weakness for chatbots.
