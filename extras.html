Kettering Health · More Facebook ads

Veterans Recruitment Ads

Result Copy 2 was the best-performing ad in all of Kettering Health’s July 2022 Facebook advertising: 44 job applications at $1.38 each, about 39× cheaper than that month’s average of $53.47. The whole Veterans campaign brought in 22% of the month’s applications on under 2% of the ad budget.

Client
Kettering Health
Goal
Recruit veterans and transitioning service members into healthcare careers.
Audience
Veterans and transitioning service members.
What I did
Wrote 11 ad variations (headline, description and primary text), each speaking to a different strength veterans bring, so the campaign could test which message landed.

Why it matters: cost per application is what the hospital paid in ads for each completed job application. At $1.38, the same budget that bought one application from an average ad bought about 39 from this one.
